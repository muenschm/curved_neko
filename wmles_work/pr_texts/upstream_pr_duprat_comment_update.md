We have reworked the local pressure gradient of the Duprat model, so the PR has three new commits:

- **Gradient averaged across element faces** before sampling (gather-scatter), since the face-normal derivative is two-valued in the C⁰ basis.
- **Vector filter:** the sampled gradient vector is filtered in time and then projected on the current flow direction. Before, the projections on the instantaneous direction were filtered and cancelled where the flow reverses.
- **No Stokes limit:** the limit on ∂p/∂s from the original implementation is not part of the paper. It capped ∂p/∂s along most of the hill wall, and the runs are stable without it, so it is removed.
- **Reversed wall shear stress:** the sign(τ_w) term of Eq. 5 of the paper is now included.

The case-file interface is unchanged. All five backends, the unit tests and the documentation are updated, and the description above is revised. On the periodic hill (CPU and one GPU), the model now sees most of the pressure gradient, and the Reynolds stresses and the skin friction in the recirculation zone are close to the reference. The separation point does not change, and the reattachment is still short of the reference.
